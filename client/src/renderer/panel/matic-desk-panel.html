What changed, and why

Two regions, not four

The panel is the conversation plus the assistant. Diagnostics are no longer resident: the machine view is one line, the inspector is absent until asked for. Nothing was deleted.

The inspector opens into the layout

Nothing reserves space for it, so nothing is left behind when it closes. Two candidates: a drawer over the conversation, and a panel beside it. Switch at the top of this page.

One inspector, two depths

Each step reads as a sentence a buyer can follow, with its raw payload one click under it, wrapped rather than clipped. The [hub]/[router]/[ticket] log is a further step down, ours alone.

Offers can be answered

Yes / No thanks sit inside the message that made the offer, and the composer says an answer is outstanding. Answering leaves a record of what you said, and it can be changed.

Evidence at the moment it matters

The stopped print service appears as the reason above the proposal, not as a row in a list nobody opens. The full machine view stays available for anyone who wants it.

Three connection states, in the user's words

Normal is a quiet line in the rail. Unreachable says what it can't do and doesn't apologise for it. None-connected reads as a configuration, not a fault — grey, no warning colour. No "hub", and no timestamps to the second.

Our words stopped reaching the user

State names, voice settings and catalog language are gone from the surface. The internal note moved to the inspector, where the text is still verbatim for us.

A decline is a result

It gets the same frame as an answer, states the limit once, and offers the next step. No apology tone, no error styling, no red.

Tokens, motion, fonts

Colour, type, spacing and motion are --matic-ui-* variables, separate from --matic-avatar-*. Only chevrons and hovers move, and prefers-reduced-motion flattens them. No webfont is requested at runtime — Barlow is named first and used if the app ships it locally.

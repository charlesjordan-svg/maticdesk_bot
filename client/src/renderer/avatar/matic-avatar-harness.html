Item 1

Twelve states × five sizes

A shared pairing is visible as a shared pairing. 160 and 96 are the full bust; 64, 44 and 32 are the head crop — a fixed source box 26,12 → 174,160 that clips identically at every one of them.
Static by default — 60 avatars animating at once costs more than the product ever will, and shapes are easier to compare still.

Item 6

The two known-weak pairings

Rendered at the sizes where they are weak, so the constraint is visible rather than only written down. This is expected behaviour, not a defect — v7's reasoning is settled.
oval + narrow at 64px — escalating fails
The ticket sits at 146,136, outside the crop box — 46% visible. The amber tip is hue, which is never a differentiator in this spec. So at 64px escalating is thinking. It is a full-bust state: 96px and up. Compare with the 96px pair below, where the ticket returns.
round + roundSmall at 64px — accept is weak
Separated only by the absence of listening's head tilt — accept's chest aperture is 11% visible under the crop, while listening's ear rings survive at 88%. Legible, but prefer the full bust. Watch them for a few seconds: the tilt is the tell.
